Seven rules

  1. Two groundsWhite #f5f3ef or espresso #390d03. Alternate in full-bleed sections. Never pure white or black.
  2. HeadlinesHigh Cruiser, always uppercase. Never body or long passages.
  3. BodyAtkinson Hyperlegible for all running text, labels and eyebrows.
  4. ScriptQuick Signature only for "Kyra Aulani", "xoxo", or one closing line.
  5. AccentOlive is the signature accent. Rose is emphasis on dark.
  6. SquareNo rounded corners, shadows or gradients.
  7. ContrastEvery text pair passes WCAG AA. Check the table below.

How it sounds

Voice: gritty-elegant

Do

  • Earthy and gritty as undercurrent, not headline
  • More elegant, more selective
  • Sharper sentences, direct, no fluff
  • Speak to established, discerning clients
  • Flag missing data: ⚑ NEEDS MANUAL INPUT

Don't

  • Casual filler
  • Invented claims, prices or testimonials
  • Rounded corners, shadows, gradients, emoji
  • Script anywhere but the name, xoxo, one closing line
  • Retired Founding Mothers palette or fonts
